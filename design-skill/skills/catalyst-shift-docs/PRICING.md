# Pricing reference

Aligned to **Strategy v6.0** (canon 2026-10-09) §2, §3 and §4. Source of truth: `Strategy and Canon/CatalystShift_Strategy_v6.0.md`. Where this file and v6.0 disagree, v6.0 wins.

> **This supersedes the v5.2 model** (a $2,500 credited assessment as the only front door, the platform priced per managed client as the only shape). Those still describe the MSP lane, which is now background (see the bottom of this file).

## The rule for services: fixed scope, priced on outcome

- **Fixed-scope, fixed-price offers**, priced per client by the teammate.
- **Priced on outcome, never on effort.** Never quote hours or a day rate as the value.
- **No public price list on any surface** — including the assessment. Never print a band, a range, or a rate card in a customer-facing doc. A proposal or SOW states the one price scoped for that client.
- **DH/O (delivery hours per outcome) is the master metric.** It prices every engagement internally; it never appears in a doc.
- **Every engagement attaches a monthly.** A **care / outcome contract** attaches at signature — never sold later.

## How to write pricing in a doc

- **Use the number the teammate gives you** for this client, and say in the doc that it is the fixed price for this scope. If you don't have one, leave `{{ENGAGEMENT_PRICE}}` in place and say so in your hand-back — never estimate one.
- **Show the care contract** as its own recurring line, starting at signature: `{{CARE_MONTHLY}} / month`.
- **State whether the assessment is credited** only if the teammate tells you. Otherwise don't mention crediting.
- **State the guarantee as defined deliverables in writing** — the deliverables and the outcomes we'll measure, agreed before a dollar changes hands. Never "keep working at no cost."
- **Never print** a band, a range, a rate card, hours, or a day rate as the value.

## Published exceptions

Partner terms stay as published exceptions — use them only when the teammate says the deal is through that partner:

- **IBPI MSP OneShare:** 15% setup discount + 10% Year 1 MRR discount + 5% annual rebate to IBPI on member-driven revenue.
- **ASCII Spark:** terms preserved as a published exception.

## MSP lane (background — use only for an MSP prospect)

v6.0 keeps the v5.2 platform model for the MSP product lane, which gets no product investment until a paying Halo customer proves it:

- Catalyst OS is priced **per managed client × use-case depth**, custom per deal, no public price list; never the use cases à la carte.
- The Halo offer has a starting shape in v6.0 §4 (setup, credited, plus a per-client Action monthly, with a client floor and a fair-use cap). Take the numbers from the teammate, never from memory.
- Front door: free **Client 360** (read-only, whole book), a Halo CSM referral, or the setup engagement; the assessment may be waived for a Halo referral.
- The managed-phase floor is **Action** (actioned intelligence) — no bare reporting tier.
