Catalyst Shift

The operating layer for MSPs.

Catalyst OS is the business-layer operating system for an MSP. A synthesis core makes your CRM, PSA, and RMM talk to each other and acts on what it finds — the client brief prepared, the churn signal caught, the proposal sent. Palantir, for the MSP business layer.

06 · Approach
06 / {{TOTAL}}

Four stages. No surprises.

  1. Listen · 30 minutes. A discovery call, not a pitch. We pressure-test the synthesis framework against how your business actually runs.
  2. Paid assessment. $2,500, fully credited. It scores how the business runs and builds your custom proposal — scope, setup, and per-client MRR.
  3. Agreement with deliverables in writing. Scope, integration plan, the deliverables and outcomes we'll measure, timeline, custom pricing, annual term.
  4. Onboarding within 5 business days. Stack credentials, baseline data, kickoff. First outcome — often the first Operator Brief — inside 30 days.
