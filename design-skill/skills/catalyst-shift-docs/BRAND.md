# Brand reference

Aligned to **Strategy v5.2** (canon 2026-06-30; amended 2026-07-03). Source of truth: `Strategy and Canon/CatalystShift_Strategy_v5.2.md`. Read this before writing any copy.

## Who Catalyst Shift is

A **product company** selling to U.S. MSPs. The product is **Catalyst OS** — the **business-layer operating system for an MSP**, built on a **synthesis core** that makes an MSP's existing stack (CRM + PSA + RMM) talk to itself and *acts* on what it finds. Catalyst OS runs the business side — exec, finance, sales, marketing — not service delivery. It's delivered as a managed service until the synthesis core is proven across ~5 customers, then it graduates toward software: a **product on a forward-deployed bridge** (the Palantir pattern, moved from national security to the commercial business layer).

**Two Co-Founders.** We sell the product, not the founders.

- **Kevin Townsend** — Co-Founder, Product & Engineering. Owns the Catalyst OS synthesis core, use-case engineering, integrations, technical delivery, infrastructure. Behind the curtain as a public face pending the Paul Cella estate engagement.
- **Lucas Dowd** — Co-Founder, Revenue. Owns the sales motion, customer relationships, partnerships, pricing, revenue operations. **The client-facing founder** — full-time on CS, no parallel work.

**Keith Maselli** stepped out of the co-founder and strategy seat (weekend 6/27–28) to an **internal build-support role**, declined equity and compensation, and is **not presented externally.** Do not introduce or name Keith in client-facing material, and do not reintroduce a third co-founder.

> **Retired (do not use):** the v4.3 founder-named differentiator — *"Kevin builds the engine that generates leads. Lucas builds the engine that closes them."* It must not appear anywhere.

## Positioning

**The differentiator (locked phrase — use verbatim when introducing the firm):**
> Catalyst OS is the business-layer operating system for an MSP — the layer the major MSP vendors have no incentive to build.

**Headline:** *"The operating layer for MSPs."*
**Banner:** *"Operator-owned intelligence, powered by synthesis."*
**Hero:** *"Your stack already holds the answer. We make it talk."*
**Tagline:** *"You built a world-class service operation. We build the business infrastructure around it."*

**The Palantir precedent (public, said out loud):** Catalyst OS is Palantir's concept — fuse scattered signal into one picture and act on it — moved from national security to the commercial business layer. Use it so buyers have a precedent: this isn't a science experiment.

**The category we're claiming:** the **MSP business layer** — the operational and revenue systems above the technical stack (ConnectWise / Kaseya / Ninja) and below the ceiling an MSP can reach by hand. Not a SaaS competitor to the PSA/RMM vendors; the **synthesis layer** that runs the business operations they don't address.

## What we sell — the platform, not the parts

We sell **the synthesis layer, priced as a platform.** **A module is a use case** — a view and a template over the common synthesis spine. The same ingested-and-synthesized data, pointed at a different question. **We do not sell the five use cases à la carte** — à la carte monetizes the parts; our moat is the sum, and selling the parts is a competitor's structural weakness, not ours.

The five use cases:

| ID | Use case | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **M.01** | **The Operator Brief** | Synthesis over CRM + PSA + RMM — recurring client intelligence, prepared before you sit down. *(The renamed QBR output. QBR is one use case, not the headline.)* |
| **M.02** | **Churn Early Warning** | A heads-up 60–90 days before a client is at risk, plus the retention play — not just a flag. |
| **M.03** | **Proposal Generation** | Proposals out the same day, priced consistently, tracked sent-to-signed. |
| **M.04** | **Pipeline Visibility** | A pipeline you can see — staged, reported weekly, forecast that calibrates. |
| **M.05** | **Authority Engine** | Market presence on a set cadence (~8–12 pieces/quarter). |

**Build state (honest).** The synthesis core exists, is reproducible, and is demoable (on dummy data until a live customer opens their stack). Delivered managed until proven across ~5 customers, then sellable as software. No finished-product claims; no public dated roadmap. Use the founding-cohort narrative; never frame customer money as "funding development."

## Critical positioning rules (v5.2)

- **Lead with synthesis.** The mechanism — "your stack, finally talking to itself," fused across CRM/PSA/RMM and acted on — is the story. "Operator-owned" is the closing promise; **AI is how we build, never the headline.**
- **Actioned, not actionable.** "Actionable intelligence is just a report. We deliver *actioned* intelligence — the work, done." The category line vs. every AI dashboard for MSPs.
- **Never name ScalePad** in customer-facing material. (Internally: "ScalePad tells you what happened; Catalyst OS does something about it.")
- **Lead with business outcomes, not technology.** Specificity (numbers, timelines, named outputs) beats vague capability claims.
- **No public price list.** Managed-phase pricing is **custom per deal** (assessment → SOW → setup + MRR), framed as an early-adopter program. Published/fixed pricing returns only at the SaaS destination. (This supersedes the v4.x "fees published and fixed" rule for the managed phase.)
- **Guarantee in writing = defined deliverables, not a refund.** Before a dollar changes hands, put the deliverables and outcomes we'll measure in writing. Do **not** promise to "keep working at no cost."
- **Honesty about build state, as advantage.** The synthesis core exists and is demoable; delivered managed until proven across ~5 customers. Radically honest, founding-cohort framing, never a finished-platform claim.
- **Portability gate.** Modules-as-use-cases ships multi-tenant, config-only, no custom code per customer at GA.

## Voice rules

- **Lead with synthesis and outcomes, not technology.** "The proposal sent, the churn signal caught" beats "AI-powered platform."
- **AI lives in methodology.** Mention AI only when describing *how* we build, never as the value prop or headline.
- **Monospace is texture, not voice.** Mono is for data, metrics, labels, system-style language. Bracket it with plain English.
- **Every sentence earns its place.** Cut filler: "transformative," "world-class," "synergize." If we say "results," we say *which* results.
- **Specificity beats abstraction.** Numbers, timelines, named outputs > vague promises.
- **Empathetic, never diagnostic.** A letter to a capable operator, not a diagnosis of a struggling one. We never imply the business is broken.
- **No exclamation marks. No emoji** unless the teammate explicitly asks.

## Target audience (v5.2 ICP)

**U.S. MSPs, owner-led.** Parameters are **revenue and managed-client count** — not endpoints or headcount.

- **Beachhead:** owner-led MSPs at **$1M–$4M** revenue, **15+ managed clients**, already attempting QBRs or a vCIO motion, on ConnectWise / Halo / Autotask + NinjaOne / Datto / Atera or equivalent.
- **Eligible:** $500K–$10M where the buyer is the owner or COO. Warm intros above/below still get sold.
- **Out of scope as a focus:** sub-$500K and above-$10M. Other verticals (DIB, MSSP) deferred.

## Sales process (4 stages)

1. **Listen / Discovery** — 30-minute friendly-MSP call. Pitch the synthesis / operating-layer framework; ask the three discovery questions ("would you pay this for this," "what would have to be true to buy," "which use case is most/least valuable").
2. **Paid assessment** — $2,500, credited; scores fit and **builds the custom proposal** (SOW + setup + MRR). The free self-serve **Scorecard** sits ahead of it as lead capture.
3. **Agreement with deliverables in writing** — scope, integration plan, the deliverables and outcomes we'll measure, timeline, custom pricing, annual term. Lucas leads; Kevin reviews technical scope.
4. **Onboarding within 5 business days.** First outcome (e.g., the first Operator Brief) within 30 days.

The Operator Brief is the default first use case unless another pain is overwhelming. Every engagement passes the **Triple-A test** — Automation, Authority, Action. Two of three, or it doesn't belong.

Surfaces are graphite (`--bg #08080B`, `--surface #101019`). Borders separate, not shadows. Section numbers render as plain `01 / Title` — no glyph prefix.
