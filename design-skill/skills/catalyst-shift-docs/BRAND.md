## Who Catalyst Shift is

A **services firm with its own platform.** We are a **Microsoft 365 partner and Copilot-focused services firm, not an MSP.** We sell **outcomes** to small operators, deliver them with our own tooling at a cost an ordinary shop can't match, and stay **accountable for the result in writing.** Catalyst OS is how we deliver. It is not what we sell.

**Positioning line (verbatim — use it when introducing the firm):**
> We sell outcomes to small businesses. We deliver them with our own tooling at a cost nobody can match, and we're accountable for the result. The software is how we do it, not what we sell.

**The spine.** Every engagement is a stop on one five-step spine, and every stop reuses the same tooling. If an offer doesn't sit on this spine, it is a favor, not a service.

| Step | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Connect** | Pull from the systems they already run, read-only |
| **Normalize** | One picture out of systems that disagree |
| **Synthesize** | What it means — per client, job, or contract |
| **Act** | The play gets run; an agent writes back, or a human executes |
| **Keep** | It keeps running, we keep watching it, they keep paying |

**Services install the product; they never replace it.** Every engagement leaves a monthly behind.

## Positioning rules (v6.0)

- **Outcomes first, accountability in writing.** Lead with the result the client gets and our accountability for it. Specificity (numbers, timelines, named outputs) beats capability claims.
- **AI is how we build, never the headline.** No "AI-powered," no "AI" in a customer-facing headline. No "general AI services" positioning in any market, on any surface — the thesis is always specific.
- **Actioned, not actionable.** "Actionable intelligence is just a report. We deliver *actioned* intelligence — the work, done."
- **Never name ScalePad** in customer-facing material.
- **No public price list on any surface**, including the assessment. A proposal states the price scoped for that client; that is not a price list.
- **Guarantee in writing = defined deliverables, not a refund.** Before a dollar changes hands, put the deliverables and the outcomes we'll measure in writing. Never "keep working at no cost."
- **No advisory-only engagements.** Every offer produces a deliverable on the spine and attaches a monthly.
- **Honest build state.** No finished-product claim, no dated public roadmap, never frame customer money as "funding development."
- **No fabricated proof** — no invented testimonials, logos, metrics, or named clients. No client or partner named before they consent in writing.

## Voice rules

- **Lead with outcomes, not technology.** "The tenant ready for Copilot, the audit evidence in one place, the proposal sent" beats "AI-powered platform."
- **AI lives in methodology.** Mention AI only when describing *how* we build, never as the value prop or headline.
- **Monospace is texture, not voice.** Mono is for data, metrics, labels, system-style language. Bracket it with plain English.
- **Every sentence earns its place.** Cut filler: "transformative," "world-class," "synergize." If we say "results," we say *which* results.
- **Specificity beats abstraction.** Numbers, timelines, named outputs > vague promises.
- **Empathetic, never diagnostic.** A letter to a capable operator, not a diagnosis of a struggling one. We never imply the business is broken.
- **No exclamation marks. No emoji** unless the teammate explicitly asks.

## How we sell

- **One front door per door, none public-priced.** DIB: a compliance conversation with someone who has been in the room (warm intros only, 60–120 day cycle). Workplace Readiness: the assessment, scoped to the door the buyer comes through. MSP on Halo: free Client 360, Halo CSM referral, or the setup engagement.
- **Deliverables and measured outcomes in writing before a dollar changes hands.**
- **No cold or purchased lists.** Nothing external ships without customer-facing validation.
- Every engagement passes the **Triple-A test** — Automation, Authority, Action. Two of three, or it doesn't belong.

## Visual system (one-paragraph version)

Dark-first, editorial. Display type **General Sans** for headlines. Body **DM Sans**. Mono **IBM Plex Mono** with `tabular-nums` and `snake_case` for system-style content. Palette: **`--signal #34E2C2`** (teal — primary accent, status, key glows, the logo mark), **`--accent #8B7CF6`** (violet — the AI/automation register, secondary CTAs), **`--warm #FF6F4D`** (coral — closing CTAs, human moments). Surfaces are graphite (`--bg #08080B`, `--surface #101019`). Borders separate, not shadows. Section numbers render as plain `01 / Title` — no glyph prefix. The section rail colors its five rows with tokens `--m01`…`--m05` (teal `#34E2C2`, pink `#FF5D8F`, violet `#8B7CF6`, amber `#F2B43C`, blue `#4DA3FF`): by default they mark the five spine steps; in an MSP-lane doc they mark M.01–M.05. The logo mark stays solid teal. All tokens live in `assets/colors_and_type.css`; shared layout chrome in `assets/doc-shell.css`.
