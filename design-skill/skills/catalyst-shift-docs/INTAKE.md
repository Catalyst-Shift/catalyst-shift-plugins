# Intake — what to ask when context is thin

When a teammate invokes this skill with a one-line ask ("make a proposal for Acme"), you need more before you can write a credible doc. Ask in **one bundled message**, not piecemeal.

## Universal questions (always ask if missing)

1. **Client / company name** — and one line on what they do.
2. **Industry / vertical** — services, industrial, GovCon, cybersecurity, agency, other.
3. **Decision-maker** — name, role, what they care about most.
4. **Stage** — first contact, post-discovery call, post-signed engagement, or delivery phase.
5. **Format preference** — HTML/PDF, PPTX, DOCX, Markdown. Default per the table in `SKILL.md`.

## Doc-specific add-ons

### Proposal
- Which tier are we proposing (01 Diagnostic / 02 Foundation / 03 Operating Layer / custom)?
- Proposed fee (use tier default unless they say otherwise).
- Specific outcomes to guarantee in writing — what does success look like by day 30 / 60 / 90?
- Any client-specific constraints (timeline, integrations, compliance)?

### SOW (statement of work)
- Has the proposal been signed? If not, write a proposal first.
- Final fee + payment schedule (default: 50% on signing, 50% on completion).
- Final guaranteed outcomes (must be specific and measurable).
- Start date.
- Who's the CS lead on the engagement (Kevin / Lucas / Keith / pair)?

### Discovery report
- When was the discovery call? Pull from transcript or notes.
- What problem(s) did the prospect surface? Use their words.
- What's the recommended path (Tier 01 / 02 / 03)?
- Did we commit to follow-up materials by a specific date?

### Sales deck
- Audience — single founder, exec team, board?
- Length — 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 slides?
- Are we leading with the diagnostic offer or the full ladder?
- Any specific case study / proof points to feature? (If none, lean on the methodology + guarantee structure.)

### Client deliverable
- Which engagement phase is this for (Discovery, Foundation, Operating Layer)?
- What's the actual content the teammate has? (The skill formats and brands; it doesn't invent the work product.)
- Internal review or client-final?

### Case study
- **Has the engagement closed and have outcomes been measured?** If no, do not write a case study — propose a "principles in practice" doc instead.
- Client approval to use their name? If not, write it anonymized.
- Specific metrics — leads/month, close rate, revenue, time-to-close, etc.

### One-pager
- What single offer is this for (Tier 01 / Tier 02 / Tier 03 / a vertical-specific play / a referral program)?
- Where will it be used — email attachment, in-person leave-behind, cold outreach?

### Internal doc
- What kind — meeting notes, retro, decision memo, planning doc?
- Audience — just the three of you, or extended team?
- Action items vs reference?

## When the teammate gives you a transcript

Read it once. Pull:
- Decision-maker name + role + what they kept coming back to
- Specific pain language they used (quote it back in the doc)
- Any explicit asks ("send us a proposal by Friday", "we need this on the board agenda")
- Any deal-breakers they mentioned (budget cap, timeline, must-haves)

Then ask only what's *not* in the transcript.

## When the teammate gives you a prior doc to remix

Read it. Note what's reusable (structure, voice, sections) and what needs to change (client name, scope, fee, dates, specific outcomes). Confirm the new client + scope, then write.
